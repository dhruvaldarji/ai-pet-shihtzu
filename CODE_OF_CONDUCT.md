# Community expectations

Be welcoming, patient, and constructive. Critique ideas and code, not people. Harassment, threats, discrimination, and sharing someone else's private information are not welcome.

The maintainer may remove harmful content or limit participation to keep the project friendly. Decisions and responses are best effort; this hobby project has no formal enforcement team.

For a concern about a public interaction, point the maintainer to it without repeating harmful content. For a sensitive concern, ask for a private contact without posting names or details; share those only after a private route is agreed. You can also use GitHub's report/block controls for platform abuse. Do not use security advisories for unrelated conduct reports.
